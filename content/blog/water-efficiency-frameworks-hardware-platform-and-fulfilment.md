# Water-efficiency programmes are systems problems, not product catalogues

A water-saving-device framework can look like a simple catalogue purchase. In practice, the commercial outcome depends on four connected systems: approved products, inventory and fulfilment, customer ordering, and evidence that devices actually reach the intended programme.

For hardware, the first question is not only unit price. Utilities need assurance that regulators, shower devices, cistern products and related components meet applicable quality and drinking-water requirements. Product substitutions must be controlled because a nominally similar item may change flow performance, installation effort or approval evidence.

The digital layer matters for the same reason. A customer survey and ordering portal has to translate a household or business need into an eligible product basket, retain reliable consent and fulfilment records, and protect customer data. It should integrate cleanly with stock, delivery and reporting processes without pretending to be the utility's operational control system.

The handoffs are where delivery risk accumulates. A useful responsibility matrix names who owns product approval, forecast changes, stock accuracy, failed deliveries, customer support, returns, security incidents and programme reporting. Acceptance tests should cover these interfaces as well as the individual products and platform.

For suppliers, the practical bid question is whether one organisation genuinely covers the whole chain. If it does, an end-to-end route is simpler. If it does not, the missing role should be explicit before partners are approached. A headline framework ceiling is not guaranteed revenue: call-offs, lot allocation and actual demand determine addressable work.

## Practical review checklist

- Separate framework ceiling, lot ceiling and expected call-off demand.
- Verify product approvals and substitution controls.
- Map survey, ordering, inventory, fulfilment and customer-data interfaces.
- Test reporting and exception handling, not only the happy path.
- Use one prime where full coverage is evidenced; add partners only for documented gaps.
