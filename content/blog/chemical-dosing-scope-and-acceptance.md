# Chemical dosing packages: qualify the interfaces before pricing the equipment

Draft only. English; Australia/global water suppliers. Intent: practical scope qualification, not a claim about a bidder or proposed team. Review28 September2026.

A dosing-package title is not a complete bill of materials. The useful first deliverable is a boundary map: which requirements belong to the equipment supplier, the controls engineer, the installer and the operating team?

## Start with what is known

Cairns' public list identifies a sodium-hypochlorite package at a reservoir, but the detailed documents require supplier access. That supports a research lead, not an assumption about tank sizes, control protocols, installation scope or eligibility. Keep those questions open until the specifications are available. [Buyer listing](https://portal.tenderlink.com/cairns/alltenders/)

## Use an acceptance matrix

For each requirement, record its source, responsible party, evidence needed, test method and acceptance authority. Useful review headings include chemical/material compatibility, dosing control, alarms, isolation, commissioning and operating handover. These are proposed diligence questions, not verified requirements of any particular tender.

Published supplier capabilities also need boundaries. A company may describe hydraulic and electrical design, monitoring and telemetry without proving that one specific product meets the reservoir duty. Ask for project-specific evidence; do not translate a broad capability page into compliance. [Example of a supplier's published water-systems capabilities](https://www.aquatecenviro.com/product/water-supply)

## One supplier or complementary specialists?

Use one supplier when it can evidence full scope and eligibility. Add specialists only for a real uncovered responsibility. Record who owns each interface before discussing workshare. Neither a draft responsibility matrix nor a contact's job title establishes willingness to bid.

## Commercial discipline

A useful integrator service could be requirements qualification and coordination of a focused technical review. The service scope, payer, fee, currency and payment trigger must be agreed separately. A tender estimate is not integrator revenue, and a future deadline does not cure a missed compulsory gate.

CTA draft: provide your technical capabilities, served regions and delivery limits for a fit review. No intake form, publication or introduction consent is implied. This content should not be relied on to rescue an imminent bid deadline.
